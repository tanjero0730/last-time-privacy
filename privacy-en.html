Privacy policy

Policy updated: 2026-10-19.

Scope and contact

This policy covers the Last Time? / 前回いつ？ Android app. Provider: tanjero.kamado. Contact: vrc.tanjero.kamado@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores item names, recorded dates, the state needed to undo a record, and optional reminder settings locally to display your records and schedule reminders. There are no accounts, ads or usage analytics SDKs. The current app does not transmit your records to a developer server and does not request internet permission.

Notifications and system features

Optional reminders request notification permission on supported Android versions. The app updates reminder schedules after events such as a restart. Your keyboard, TalkBack, Android notifications and file picker, and store services such as Google Play operate under their providers' policies and your device settings.

Backup and restore

You can choose to export item names and recorded dates to a file. Reminder settings are not included. If you choose a cloud provider in the system file picker, that provider handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy. Check where you save files and with whom you share them. Before a restore replaces the current records, the app also keeps a local copy of the previous records in its private storage.

Retention and deletion

The developer does not retain server copies of your records. You can delete individual items in the app, but an earlier local recovery copy may still contain them. To remove all local app data, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Exported backup files must be deleted separately. The developer cannot remotely access or delete files on your device.

Support and changes

If you email support, your message and reply address are used to respond to your request. You do not need to include personal information or your task records. This policy and the store declarations will be updated if data handling changes.
